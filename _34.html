About Infintin - Pioneering Technology

Reimagining Electric Powertrains

Our Vision

To enable sustainable mobility and industrial automation by delivering high-efficiency electric motors completely independent of rare-earth materials like Neodymium and Dysprosium.

Our Technical Approach

  • •To pioneer sustainable electric drive solutions by delivering high‑efficiency, rare‑earth‑free motors that reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions across mobility and industrial applications.
  • •To empower industries and communities with resilient, affordable, and environmentally responsible motor technologies that eliminate rare‑earth dependency and accelerate the transition to clean electrification.
  • •To advance innovation in electric drives by creating high‑performance, rare‑earth‑free motors that enable sustainable growth, strengthen supply chains, and contribute to global decarbonization.

National Atmanirbhar Commitment

Establishing an end-to-end R&D and manufacturing hub in Tamil Nadu, aligned with "Make in India" initiatives to secure domestic EV supply chains.

Industry Challenges

The Problem

Rapid electrification and climate mandates have exposed critical inefficiencies and rare-earth supply chain bottlenecks across industries.

Energy Waste

40% of all building energy is consumed by HVAC systems — powered by induction motors that waste nearly a third of every unit of electricity through inefficiency.

Inefficient Induction Motors ~30% Power Wasted
Supply Bottleneck

High-efficiency PMSM motors depend on rare earth magnets. China's April 2025 export controls have disrupted India's supply chain.

Export Restrictions Critical Material Dependency
Regulatory Risk

Non-compliant motors will be locked out of the market as stricter efficiency standards and import norms are enforced.

Strict Efficiency Norms Loss of Market Access
Urgent OEM Challenge

For HVAC OEMs and industrial motor manufacturers, rising energy costs and ESG mandates are turning this into an urgent commercial risk.

ESG Mandates Rising Operational Costs
